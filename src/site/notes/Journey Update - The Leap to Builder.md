# Journey Update: From Researcher to AI Builder 🚀
**Date:** Friday, February 20, 2026
**Status:** Digital Garden Update / Portfolio Entry

## 📍 The Starting Line: January 2026 (The "Researcher" Phase)
Only a month ago, my workflow was centered on **Agent-Led Research**.
- **The Loop:** Every morning, an agent would dive into EPA regulations, methane flaring stats, and competitor moves for @strandedcompute.
- **The Output:** Structured notes and X threads.
- **The Learning:** I was mastering the art of the *Deep Prompt*. I was learning how to treat AI as a junior analyst, pushing it for sources and technical depth. But I was still "outside" the tech—observing and documenting.

## ⚙️ The Strategic Shift: Early February (The "Architect" Phase)
The chaos began to crystallize into the **3-Box Logic**:
- **Box 1 (GasCo):** Energy Harvesting.
- **Box 2 (ComputeCo):** Hardware Density.
- **Box 3 (OpCo):** The Trust Layer.
This wasn't just research anymore; it was an **Operational Framework**. I started integrating local models via [[Ollama]] to keep my "Operational Alpha" private. The vault became my second brain, not just a scratchpad.

## ⌨️ The CLI Era: Mid-February (The "Pilot" Phase)
I moved from web interfaces to the **Command Line**.
- **Tools:** [[Gemini CLI]], [[Termux]], and [[Ollama]].
- **The Shift:** I realized that if I wanted to build at the speed of AI, I had to live where the agents live. The terminal became my cockpit. I started automating the vault itself—auto-syncing to [[GitHub]], auto-generating daily briefs, and managing my digital garden from a prompt.

## 🏗️ The Current Leap: Late February (The "Builder" Phase)
Today, I'm not just researching the industry; I'm **contributing to it**.
- **The Move:** Full send on [[OpenClaw]] (PR #22758).
- **The Vibe:** Using agents to find "pain points" (like missing auto-update configs) and shipping PRs to massive open-source projects.
- **The Philosophy:** "Vibe-coding" isn't about being lazy; it's about being an **Agent Orchestrator**. I use AI to handle the boilerplate, the syntax, and the tests, while I focus on the high-level logic and "pain discovery."

## 📈 What I've Learned (The Hard Way)
1. **Context is King:** A vault is only as good as its links. [[Double Brackets]] are the connective tissue of my digital twin.
2. **Local vs. Cloud:** High-privacy local models (Ollama) for the *Alpha*; Massive cloud models (Gemini Ultra) for the *Drafts*.
3. **Ship or Die:** Research is a liability until it's a contribution. A merged PR is worth 1,000 research notes.
