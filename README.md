# 🧠 Brain Fartz Mobile

> **"Capturing chaotic ideas and turning them into organized notes."**

Brain Fartz Mobile is an experimental, AI-integrated [[Obsidian]] vault designed for high-velocity research, operational deep-dives, and digital gardening. This vault serves as a digital twin for managing the intersection of energy, compute, and decentralized finance.

---

## 🏗️ The 3-Box Operational Framework
The core strategy of this vault is grounded in the **3-Box Logic**:

- **Box 1 (GasCo):** Methane capturing and Virtual Power Plants (VPP). Turning flared gas into decentralized energy.
- **Box 2 (ComputeCo):** High-density AI infrastructure. Liquid-cooled NVIDIA Blackwell racks and modular pods.
- **Box 3 (OpCo):** The Token-Oracle. Using [[Chainlink]] and [[Pyth]] to verify inference and secure "Compute Bonds."

---

## 📂 Vault Structure

- **`05-AI-Daily/`**: Daily snapshots of the AI landscape (news, tools, and local model releases).
- **`Business Intel/`**: Deep-dive reports on operational mechanics, competitors like [[CoreWeave]], and partners like [[Injective]].
- **`Groks-Score-Last-30-days-AI/`**: Research improvement reports and optimized agent prompting strategies.
- **`last 30 days/`**: Comprehensive research on new AI stocks, tools, and local [[Ollama Models]].
- **`src/`**: The digital garden engine (Eleventy-based) used to publish these notes.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Integration
- **Local Models:** Heavy focus on [[Ollama Models]] (Llama 3.2, Phi-4, GLM-5) for privacy-first research.
- **Agentic Workflow:** Powered by [[Gemini CLI]] for autonomous research, file management, and Git synchronization.
- **Digital Garden:** Notes are published via a custom Eleventy/NJC pipeline.

---

## 📝 Conventions
- **Checkboxes:** Used for task tracking within notes.
- **Double Brackets:** All core concepts are linked via `[[Double Brackets]]`.
- **Punchy & Experimental:** Entries prioritize high signal-to-noise ratios.

## 🚀 Automation
This vault is maintained by a specialized AI agent running via **Gemini CLI**.

- **Daily Reports:** Automatically generated every 24 hours in `05-AI-Daily/`.
- **Market Research:** Deep-dives performed monthly and housed in `last 30 days/`.
- **Git Sync:** Changes are continuously committed and pushed to GitHub for Windows/Android cross-compatibility.
