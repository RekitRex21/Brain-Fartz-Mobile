### 2. [[Hardware]]: Dielectric Fluid Logistics
**Research Summary**
- **The "New Oil":** Dielectric fluid (synthetic hydrocarbon) is the lifeblood of 2026 AI cooling. Sourcing it in rural KS/OK is a logistical challenge.
- **Suppliers:** Major players like Shell, Castrol, and Engineered Fluids have ramped up production, but distribution hubs are coastal.
- **Rural Logistics:** We rely on the same "Bobtail" tanker network that delivers diesel to farms. We repurpose the supply chain.
- **Volume:** A 1MW immersion pod requires ~1,000 gallons of fluid. Leaks/evaporation are minimal (<1% per year) in closed systems.
- **Thermal Stability:** Fluids must handle the -10°F Kansas winters and 110°F summers without degrading.
- **Flammability:** 2026 fluids have high flash points (>300°C), reducing fire risk compared to mineral oils of the past.
- **Cost:** ~$15-$20 per gallon. A full tank is a $20k CapEx line item.
- **Filtration:** The "Kidney Loop" filtration system is critical. Rural dust is the enemy; if it gets into the fluid, dielectric strength drops.
- **Local Partners:** We partner with local lubricant distributors (ag co-ops) to stock backup fluid drums within 50 miles.
- **Recycling:** Used dielectric fluid can be re-refined, creating a circular economy loop.
