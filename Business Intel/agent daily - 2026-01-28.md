### Topic 6: Direct-to-Chip (D2C) Cooling in the Wild

#### Research Summary
*   **Tech Shift:** D2C cooling (circulating fluid directly on the CPU/GPU) is moving from "exotic" to "standard" for high-density chips (>500W TDP) [Vertiv].
*   **Ruggedization:** D2C is ideal for harsh/dusty oilfield environments because it seals the chip away from the air. Less need for pristine air filtration.
*   **Efficiency:** D2C captures ~70-80% of heat to liquid, reducing the fan power needed (PUE improvement).
*   **Maintenance:** Requires "coolant monitoring" (leaks, quality), which adds a layer of complexity for remote sites, but reduces mechanical failure points (fewer fans) [Future Bridge].
